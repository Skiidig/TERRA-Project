# Project brief

TERRA is an immersive architectural experience for a creative development portfolio. The subject is a tropical modern villa shaped by shade, ventilation, and a close relationship with its landscape. Combine the composed scale of a diorama with realistic materials and lighting. Use English editorial content and cinematic camera movement to guide exploration on desktop and mobile.

## MVP

- A responsive editorial site with one persistent WebGL canvas and a usable fallback.
- A procedural villa with physically based materials and lighting, framed as an architectural diorama.
- An intro, orbit interaction, and scroll driven camera transitions with reduced motion support.
- Measured load and rendering performance on desktop and mobile.

## After the MVP

Replace the procedural villa with optimized GLB/glTF assets when the composition is stable. Explore advanced features such as interior walkthroughs and alternate lighting conditions after the MVP is complete.
